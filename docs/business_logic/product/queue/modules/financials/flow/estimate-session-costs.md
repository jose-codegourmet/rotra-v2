# Flow: estimate session costs and keep inventory

## Before the session

1. In session setup, the operator reaches the **Financials** step.
2. Optionally enter the court fee per hour per court. The step shows courts × length = court cost.
3. Optionally enter the venue's entrance fee per head (a misc cost, prefilled from the venue or club default).
4. Pick shuttlecock brands from inventory and the number of tubes for each. If a brand is missing, open **Manage inventory**, add it, and return to the same draft.
5. Payment per player — the full amount each player pays — is **Break-even** by default, worked out automatically. Optionally choose **Custom amount** and enter a set amount per head; the step shows the break-even amount and whether that amount makes a profit or a loss.
6. Read the estimate: courts, shuttlecocks, entrance fees, total cost, break-even, what players pay, what you'd collect, and the result (profit, loss, or breaks even).
7. Review below on the same page, then save the session online.

Every value is optional. Skipping the step leaves the session without an estimate.

## After the session

1. Open Inventory from the home sidebar.
2. Use − and + to bring stock in line with what was used. ROTRA does not deduct it automatically.
3. Watch for **Running low** items (2 or fewer left).

Collecting the fee from players happens outside ROTRA. Nothing in this flow records payments.
