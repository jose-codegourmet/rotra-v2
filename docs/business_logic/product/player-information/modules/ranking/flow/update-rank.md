# Flow: update skill rank and Player leaderboards

**Last verified:** 2026-11-18 for calendar leaderboards and skill-rank progress.

## Roster assessment

The Queue Master confirms the local level during roster preparation. It locks at session start or when a late arrival is added. Only a new player's first level for the night seeds their shared rating. Subsequent local assessments serve that session.

## Valid session finalization

1. Reconcile the session online and confirm Session done.
2. Record each valid standard match once. Both players affected by a mid-game substitution are excluded from long-term performance for that match.
3. Count wins and losses from full participation toward the annual, monthly and weekly lists, using the original time of play. A late sync can change a previous period's final places and the movement marks shown now.
4. Maintain skill-rank progress using its own 90-day window and approved rating policy. A player's leaderboard places are available independently of skill-rank calculation.

Neither read depends on the club publishing its standings. The exact rating constants remain in #247. [Ranking rules](../README.md).

## Calendar rollover and inactivity

Start a new year on January 1, a new month on day 1 and a new week on Monday, at 00:00 Asia/Manila. Compare places against the preceding calendar period. After 90 days without a counted game, preserve the old skill label with **Needs reassessment**. A player's annual wins still count until the calendar year ends.
