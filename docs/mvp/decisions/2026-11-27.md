### D-2026-11-27-01 — Payments: a player can play free, even after being marked paid
- **Decision status:** approved (owner, November 27, 2026, screenshot of Payments: "make a 'excluded' feature or free so it doesnt count them during the 'fair share' mode"; then artifact comment "the QM can still revert them back to free" and, in chat, "I still cant put them to free even if I marked them paid … the payments arent so strict at all … this only serves as a guide only").
- **Implementation status:** implemented in the mock app and real mode (November 27, 2026): `session_players.is_free` (migration `20261127000100_session_player_free`, applied to the shared database November 27, 2026 with `prisma migrate deploy`), `setPlayerFree` and `POST /api/sessions/[id]/payments/free`, the break-even share over payers in `estimateSessionCost` (decided-math test), payments totals, Mark all paid, the public payment status, and the offline outbox command `payments.free`. The amount due is not recomputed offline until the next sync. Not browser-checked in real mode.
- **Ticket:** none (done straight from the owner's request; [free players log](../../mockup-changes/2026-11-27-queue-free-players-in-payments.md), IN MOCK APP).
- **Roadmap:** MVP — [Queue](../workstreams/queue.md)
- **Decision:**
  - **Free.** The Queue Master can mark any roster player **Free** from their card (Payment sheet › **Plays free** switch) and switch it off again. Per session only; no player record changes.
  - **Break-even share.** A free player owes ₱0 and leaves the head count: total cost ÷ paying players, rounded up, at least 1 payer. A custom amount per player is unchanged; costs (entrance fee included) stay the same.
  - **Totals and filters.** Paid reads X of the paying players with "N free" apart; Still due, to reimburse and Mark all paid skip free players; collected and against costs are unchanged. A **Free · N** filter tab always shows, Free · 0 included (owner, November 27, 2026: "you forgot about the free tab").
  - **Paid players can be set free.** Payments are a guide only, so turning on Plays free for a player with cash recorded clears their amount received. Nothing goes to "to reimburse". Recording cash for a free player makes them a payer again.
  - **Exception.** A slot that carries cash from a slot transfer can't be set free (clearing it would undo the other player's transfer).
- **Open:** none.

### D-2026-11-27-02 — Win chance on Queue Master match cards, Upset, and Even game by win chance
- **Decision status:** approved (owner, November 27, 2026: "I want to introduct the feature called Odds in the Queue app · this will make it easier for the QMs to know what the odds will be per match"; shown to the "QM", on "match cards . all match cards . small indicator"; "it should affect the match making"; "yeah come up with a better name please"; "can you put the percentage per pair"; then "please update mockup and real mode · mockup is real ssource of truth"). The formula is the one proposed in chat and built in the mockup.
- **Implementation status:** built in the mockup, the mock app and real-mode services (`@rotra/data` match/winChance.rules.ts; not run in a browser). Real mode has no leaderboard place or direction yet (rank calculation is not real), so those two signals are 0 there.
- **Ticket:** needed (see the [mockup log](../../mockup-changes/2026-11-27-queue-win-chance.md)).
- **Roadmap:** MVP — [Queue](../workstreams/queue.md)
- **Decision:**
  - **Name.** The feature is **Win chance** (not "Odds"). A pair at 65% or more is **Favored**. A completed match the Favored pair lost is an **Upset**.
  - **Where.** Queue Master only. Each pair's percent on draft tiles and Main Queue cards (beside the pair; under the names on compact tiles); one chip "39% · 61%", left pair first, on court cards and finished matches, with an Upset pill when it applies. Never on the public page, in the Player app, in results or in exports.
  - **Formula.** Each player has a form rating in level steps: level (none = 4); club win rate, (rate − 50%) × 2, capped ±1, where a guest or a player with fewer than 10 decided games at the club counts as 50%; +0.25 when tonight is their best day (the weekday with their highest win rate and at least 3 games); ±0.25 for the top or bottom quarter of the club leaderboard; ±0.25 for rising or falling; 0.25 × (wins − losses) tonight, capped ±1; ±0.25 per Upset win or loss tonight, capped ±0.5; −0.25 per game in a row before the match, 2 at most; −0.5 with a handicap tonight. A team is the average of its players. Win chance = 1 ÷ (1 + 10^((opponents − own team) ÷ 2)), kept between 5% and 95%.
  - **Finished matches** are read from the lasting signals only (level, club win rate, best day, leaderboard), so an Upset mark never moves later. Nothing is stored on the match.
  - **Match making.** Even game (the third check of [D-2026-11-25-01](2026-11-25.md#d-2026-11-25-01)) splits teams by the form rating instead of the level plus tonight's results. The ladder order, and the Chill and Fair easy-game shares, are unchanged. Suggest (one match) splits its teams by the same form rating (owner, November 27, 2026: "Yes, use win chance"); Main Queue regeneration keeps the assigned level.
- **Supersedes:** in D-2026-11-25-01, "No long-term win rate and no leaderboard place" for Even game.
- **Open:** leaderboard place and direction in real mode wait for rank math ([open decisions](../../business_logic/product/open-decisions.md)). The session record of a completed session does not show the chip yet.
- **References:** [queueing › Win chance](../../business_logic/product/queue/modules/queueing/README.md#win-chance), [mockup log](../../mockup-changes/2026-11-27-queue-win-chance.md).
- **Amended by** [D-2026-11-27-03](#d-2026-11-27-03): finished games in the Player app show Win chance with the breakdown.

### D-2026-11-27-03 — Win chance in the Player app, with the breakdown dialog
- **Decision status:** approved (owner, November 27, 2026: "these places should have win rates · win rates should be clickable with exaplanation in a dialog · and sshould alsso see in player profile matches"; for the Player app the owner chose "Yes, full breakdown").

Leaderboard place and direction stay 0 in real mode until rank math.
- **Ticket:** needed (see the [mockup log](../../mockup-changes/2026-11-27-player-win-chance.md)).
- **Roadmap:** MVP — [Player Information](../workstreams/player.md)
- **Decision:**
  - **Amends** the "Queue Master only" rule of [D-2026-11-27-02](#d-2026-11-27-02). Finished games in the Player app show Win chance: the Games list, the profile's recent games and match history (own and other players' profiles), the session matches dialog, and the match page.

  - **Clickable.** On the match page the pill and both team chips open the **Win chance** dialog (P-WIN-CHANCE): per pair the percent and Favored, per player the form rating and its rows (level, club win rate, best day, club leaderboard, leaderboard direction), then the team average. In lists the pill is display only; the court still opens the match page.

  - **Still never** on the public session page, in results slides or in exports.
- **Open:** none new; leaderboard place and direction in real mode wait for rank math, as in D-2026-11-27-02.
