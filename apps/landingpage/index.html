How ranking works

A rank you earn at partner clubs.

ROTRA ranks badminton players by the games they win at ROTRA Partner clubs. Nobody rates themselves. Clubs assess, and wins decide.

See it as a storyFor club owners
Two parts

The club's call, then the shared rank

At the club

Partners assess, locally

Before each night the partner's Queue Master sets every player's level for the night from the nine-level ladder. That's what the club uses to make fair games. Levels lock when the session starts.

For a new player, that first level is where their ROTRA rank starts.

Across every club

Wins, weighted by who you beat

Before each counted game ROTRA compares the two teams. After it, every player moves up or down. Score doesn't change the step in v1.

After your first night, only wins and losses move your rank.

The rules

The fine print, in plain words

What counts

Valid singles and doubles games from finished sessions at partner clubs. Fun games and invalid games don't count.

Provisional

New players are Provisional for their first games, so their rank settles fast. No leaderboard spot yet.

90 days

Your rank reads the last 90 days. Stop playing and it shows Needs reassessment until you're back.

One rank

Singles and doubles share one rank, at every partner club.

The ladder

Nine levels, three groups

Your level is read off your rank. Nobody hands it out.
