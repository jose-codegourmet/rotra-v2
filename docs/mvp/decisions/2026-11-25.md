### D-2026-11-25-01 — Match making factors for Generate matches: Whose turn, Fresh faces, Even game
- **Decision status:** approved (owner, November 25, 2026).
- **Implementation status:** not started (mockup only).
- **Ticket:** needed (see the [mockup log](../../mockup-changes/2026-11-25-queue-match-making-factors.md)).
- **Roadmap:** MVP — [Queue](../workstreams/queue.md)
- **Decision:** Generate matches uses three checks in this priority order. It is a ladder, not a points score: a higher check always wins over a lower one.
  1. **Whose turn** (fair turns win over close games). Fewest games (played plus planned load, as before), then longest wait or rest, plus an **exhaustion rule**: a player who has played **2 games in a row** sits out at least one before Generate matches picks them again (**Hard** mode allows **3**). "In a row" is a streak of their matches with no match started in between that they sat out; sitting out one started match resets the streak. If there are too few players otherwise, rested players are used first, and exhausted players only to make the match possible.
  2. **Fresh faces.** A player is not put with or against anyone they partnered or faced in either of **their last 2 games** (completed, or On court or Playing). A strong avoid: it is broken only when no valid match is possible otherwise.
  3. **Even game.** Teams are split by level adjusted by **tonight's results only**: effective level = level + 0.25 × (wins − losses tonight), capped at ±1 level. No long-term win rate and no leaderboard place (rank math is still open). The Chill and Fair easy-game percentages stay as before.
  - Each draft tile's reason line says what drove it, in a few words, for example "Rested 2 matches", "Avoided repeat partners", "Couldn't avoid a repeat".
  - It applies in all five Generate matches tabs, Catch Up included. A bagging pair still wins over Fresh faces for that pair ([D-2026-11-11-11](2026-11-11.md#d-2026-11-11-11)).
- **Not in MVP / Out of scope:** long-term win rate or leaderboard place in team balance (rank math stays [open](../../business_logic/product/open-decisions.md)). Auto Queue (court filling), Suggest, Add match and Make match are unchanged.
- **Supersedes:** the [previous prototype reference heuristic](../../business_logic/product/queue/modules/queueing/README.md#previous-prototype-reference-heuristic) for Generate matches (kept in the module as history).
- **References:** [queueing module › Match making factors](../../business_logic/product/queue/modules/queueing/README.md#match-making-factors), [match queue screens](../../screens/product/modules/queue/match-queue.md), [mockup log](../../mockup-changes/2026-11-25-queue-match-making-factors.md), [architecture: shared queue rules](../../architecture.md#shared-queue-rules-go-and-webassembly).

### D-2026-11-25-02 — "Almost done" switch on a Playing court card
- **Decision status:** approved (owner, November 25, 2026).
- **Implementation status:** not started (mockup only).
- **Ticket:** needed (see the [mockup log](../../mockup-changes/2026-11-25-queue-almost-done-switch.md)).
- **Roadmap:** MVP — [Queue](../workstreams/queue.md)
- **Decision:**
  - Shown only on a court whose match is **Playing** (not Getting ready, not a free court): a row under the players with the label **Almost done** and a switch. Optional; the Queue Master flips it after asking the players.
  - While it is on, the court's time line reads **Almost done** in place of the expected finish (Finishing soon, Ends in about N min and the rest), in the same style as Finishing soon. Plan Ahead treats those players as free soon with 0 minutes left (top of the tie order), whatever the time estimate says.
  - It resets by itself at **Match done**, and whenever the match leaves Playing (Pause match, Mark invalid).

- **Ticket:** needed (see the [mockup log](../../mockup-changes/2026-11-25-queue-handicap-tonight.md)).

- **Decision:**

### D-2026-11-25-04 — Four leaderboard periods for ROTRA and clubs, shared places, an always-on podium, full names on leaderboards
- **Decision status:** approved (owner, November 25, 2026, with answers to follow-up questions the same day).
- **Implementation status:** built in the mock app and real-mode services ([#1106](https://github.com/jose-codegourmet/rotra-v2/issues/1106), PR [#1107](https://github.com/jose-codegourmet/rotra-v2/pull/1107)); real mode not checked in a browser. Player mockup: [log](../../mockup-changes/2026-11-25-player-leaderboard-podium.md). Queue mockup: [log](../../mockup-changes/2026-11-25-queue-leaderboard-podium.md).
- **Ticket:** needed
- **Roadmap:** MVP — [Player](../workstreams/player.md), [Queue](../workstreams/queue.md), [Ranking](../workstreams/ranking.md)
- **Amends:** in part [D-2026-11-16-05](2026-11-16.md#d-2026-11-16-05) (three lists become four; "higher win rate breaks a tie, then the account nickname" no longer decides a place; leaderboards no longer mask names; the open point on the Queue session page is settled), and the leaderboard part of the masking rules of [D-2026-11-09-07](2026-11-09.md#d-2026-11-09-07) and [D-2026-11-11-11](2026-11-11.md#d-2026-11-11-11).
- **Decision:**
  - **Four periods, for ROTRA and for clubs.** In the owner's words: "LEADERBOARDS needs to be Annually , Quarterly , Monthly and Weekly Same with club and rotra". The lists are **Top annual, Top quarterly, Top monthly and Top weekly**, on the ROTRA leaderboard and on a new **club leaderboard**.
  - **Quarters:** Q1 January to March, Q2 April to June, Q3 July to September, Q4 October to December, each starting at 00:00 Asia/Manila (owner choice). A quarter is compared with last quarter, with the same movement marks as the other lists. The period label reads, for example, "Oct to Dec 2026".
  - **Full names and pictures.** In the owner's words: "please do not hide the full name". Every leaderboard (ROTRA, club and session) shows the player's full name and picture to everyone, signed in or not, whatever their profile visibility (Private or Always private). This overrides the masking rule **for leaderboards only**; profiles, member lists, search outside the leaderboard and the rest keep their current rules (owner choice). Because full names are visible there, the leaderboard's name search finds these players too. A row whose profile the viewer may not open still shows the name and picture and opens nothing (mockup).
  - **The podium is always shown.** In the owner's words: "I want the leaderboards to always show the PODIUM". With fewer than three places, the empty places keep empty steps; with an empty list, the podium shows three empty steps above the existing empty message (owner choice).
  - **Shared places.** Players with the same wins **and** the same win rate share a place. Numbering runs without gaps (1, 1, 2), like the Queue session standings ([D-2026-11-18-05](2026-11-18.md#d-2026-11-18-05), amended November 19). Inside a shared place, players are ordered by the account nickname (full name when there is none), then ID (owner choice). This replaces "win rate breaks a tie, then nickname" as a place decider.
  - **Stacked podium places.** In the owner's words: "multiple people can be in the podium per place as long as it is stacked". Several players in one podium place are shown stacked on that step: overlapping pictures, the names listed, and "+N" for the rest.
  - **Shared place dialog.** In the owner's words: "if multiple people are in a place of the podium. clickin it should open a dialog on who's in that place". The dialog lists everyone in that place: picture, full name, nickname, wins and win rate; each row opens the profile as leaderboard rows do today.
  - **Club leaderboard** (owner choices): counts only standard-match wins in that club's own sessions, and anyone who played there can appear. Same four periods, top 50, and the same podium and list design. It sits on the Player app's club page (the page with Members, Upcoming sessions and Next up) as a Leaderboard card with the period tabs, the podium and the list, in the ROTRA leaderboard's look, kept to a reasonable size. The mockup's choice: the podium and the next 7 players, then **See all** to a club leaderboard page (`/clubs/[clubId]/leaderboard`) with the whole top 50.
  - **Profiles:** the profile's "one box per list the player is in" becomes up to four boxes (quarterly added). Club places are not shown on a profile.
  - **Queue session leaderboard.** In the owner's words: "the session leaderboards should be one only. and it is for the current session". In the Queue app, the session console's Leaderboard is a podium and a list for the current session only, with no period tabs.
  - **Queue Leaderboards page.** A new **Leaderboards** page in the Queue app's left nav, with **Club** and **ROTRA** tabs, each with the four periods.
  - **Data:** no new table. Every list is computed on read from `player_match_facts`; a club list uses the facts whose session belongs to the club ([database design](../../database_design/product/player-information/modules/ranking.md#period-leaderboards)).
- **Open:**
  - **Top 50 with a tie at the cut-off.** The mockup lists the first 50 players plus anyone who shares the 50th player's place, so a list can run a little over 50. Not confirmed by the owner.
  - **Empty podium step look.** The mockup shows an empty dashed circle and "No one yet" on a step no one holds. Not confirmed by the owner.
  - **Privacy Policy wording.** The Player app's Privacy Policy text says Private players' names are masked for visitors; it does not yet say leaderboards always show the full name and picture. Legal copy is under review ([D-2026-11-18-02](2026-11-18.md#d-2026-11-18-02)), so it is not changed here.
  - Share images of a leaderboard place and the Admin "On leaderboard" labels are unchanged.

### D-2026-11-25-05 — Real-mode session page follows the mock: full roster and waitlist names when signed in, Payment per player, match name in View match
- **Decision status:** approved (owner, November 25, 2026: "follow mock" on all four points).
- **Implementation status:** written in the real-mode services and the session page; not type-checked, built or run against a database yet.
- **Ticket:** [403 · F07.62](../real-mode-ticket-drafts.md) (the November 25 note listed these as open questions).
- **Roadmap:** MVP — [Queue](../workstreams/queue.md), [Player](../workstreams/player.md)
- **Decision:** the real-mode public session page (`/s/[slug]`) matches the mock app on four points.
  - **Roster cap, signed in.** A signed-in Player sees the whole roster, whether or not they hold a slot. Visitors keep the 12-tile cap. (Before: real mode removed the cap only for a Player holding an unreleased slot.)
  - **Waitlist names, signed in.** The signed-in name rule (registered and unclaimed players' full name, nickname and RTR ID; guests masked; always-private players masked for everyone but themselves) also applies to the waitlist. Visitors stay masked.
  - **Payment per player, everyone.** Details › Payment shows in real mode for visitors and signed-in players: the session's payment per player (custom amount or break-even share, integer centavos), "Free" when 0, omitted when no estimate is saved. It is the same amount due as the Queue Master's Payments and Share payment status. The cost lines and the estimate are never sent.
  - **Match name in View match.** Each waiting match in the signed-in Queue carries its name ("Mixed Doubles", [D-2026-11-19-07](2026-11-19.md#d-2026-11-19-07)) by the shared match category rule. A player's gender is the one on their ROTRA profile ([D-2026-11-19-01](2026-11-19.md#d-2026-11-19-01)); a guest, a club-only record or a player with no gender counts as not given, which makes the match Genderless, as in the mock rule.
- **Open:**
  - Real mode stores gender on the ROTRA profile only, so the Queue Master's own live console (which reads no gender in real mode) and this page can name the same match differently until the console reads it too.
