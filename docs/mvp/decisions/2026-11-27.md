### D-2026-11-27-01 — Payments: a player can play free, even after being marked paid
- **Decision status:** approved (owner, November 27, 2026, screenshot of Payments: "make a 'excluded' feature or free so it doesnt count them during the 'fair share' mode"; then artifact comment "the QM can still revert them back to free" and, in chat, "I still cant put them to free even if I marked them paid … the payments arent so strict at all … this only serves as a guide only").
- **Implementation status:** implemented in the mock app and real mode (November 27, 2026): `session_players.is_free` (migration `20261127000100_session_player_free`, applied to the shared database November 27, 2026 with `prisma migrate deploy`), `setPlayerFree` and `POST /api/sessions/[id]/payments/free`, the break-even share over payers in `estimateSessionCost` (decided-math test), payments totals, Mark all paid, the public payment status, and the offline outbox command `payments.free`. The amount due is not recomputed offline until the next sync. Not browser-checked in real mode.
- **Ticket:** none (done straight from the owner's request; [free players log](../../mockup-changes/2026-11-27-queue-free-players-in-payments.md), IN MOCK APP).
- **Roadmap:** MVP — [Queue](../workstreams/queue.md)
- **Decision:**
  - **Free.** The Queue Master can mark any roster player **Free** from their card (Payment sheet › **Plays free** switch) and switch it off again. Per session only; no player record changes.
  - **Break-even share.** A free player owes ₱0 and leaves the head count: total cost ÷ paying players, rounded up, at least 1 payer. A custom amount per player is unchanged; costs (entrance fee included) stay the same.
  - **Totals and filters.** Paid reads X of the paying players with "N free" apart; Still due, to reimburse and Mark all paid skip free players; collected and against costs are unchanged. A **Free · N** filter tab shows while anyone is free.
  - **Paid players can be set free.** Payments are a guide only, so turning on Plays free for a player with cash recorded clears their amount received. Nothing goes to "to reimburse". Recording cash for a free player makes them a payer again.
  - **Exception.** A slot that carries cash from a slot transfer can't be set free (clearing it would undo the other player's transfer).
- **Open:** none.
