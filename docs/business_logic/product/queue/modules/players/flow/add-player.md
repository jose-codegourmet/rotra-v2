# Flow: add and confirm a player

1. Open **Add player** from preparation, or **Add late player** from the live console. The sheet names the target session.
2. Search **Saved players** by name, nickname, or RTR ID. Pick one who is not already in the session.
3. If they are new to the club, look them up in **Player Information** (online only) by their full RTR ID or full email; there is no name search here ([D-2026-11-16-04](../../../../../../mvp/decisions/2026-11-16.md#d-2026-11-16-04)). A registered (claimed) profile needs no identity check and is added in one tap at its Player Information level. Another club's unclaimed player comes back with no name, picture or level: type the club's own name for them and choose a level. Confirm with the player before adding them.
4. Otherwise choose **New player**: name, optional nickname, optional email. Without an email they are saved as a guest. With one they are saved as an unclaimed player and get an RTR ID, or are linked to the ROTRA player who already has that email. Offline they are marked Lookup pending until the device syncs.
5. Choose the level for this club. It starts at the Player Information suggestion when there is one. In a live session it locks immediately.
6. **Confirm player slot.** The player joins the roster and the club's saved list. In preparation they start not present; in a live session they start present.

## Offline arrival

An unexpected player can join a downloaded session as a new player with name, level, and optional email. Player Information lookup waits for reconnection; RTR IDs are not generated offline.

## Returning incomplete profile

Collect missing details or issue a registration link. The link claims/completes a profile; it does not reserve or confirm session slots. See [registration](../../../../player-information/modules/identity/flow/claim-and-recover.md).
