# Flow: activate, expire, and renew a club license

1. The platform owner has added the club in Admin, with its own monthly and yearly price or as a free club.
2. The platform owner collects payment outside ROTRA for monthly or discounted yearly access at that club's price. A free club pays nothing.
3. In Admin, activate/renew the club license for the chosen period. The amount recorded is the club's price for that plan unless the platform owner changes it, or ₱0 for a free club.
4. The club operates with one active session at a time.
5. On expiry, block ordinary Queue app access. Permit an already-active session to finish and sync.
6. Mark public session pages stale and begin the six-month deletion countdown.
7. If renewed within that period, restore pages and cancel deletion. A subsequent expiry starts a new countdown.
8. If expiry continues for six months, delete the public pages while preserving player match history and earned rankings.

Renewal after pages have already been deleted requires further policy; no restoration promise is made here.
